# Charter — w08-respaldo · "Respaldo: qué hacer después del fraude"

> The constitution of this product. When my agent and I disagree about a feature at 2am, this is the referee. Written BEFORE the first line of code.

**What it exists to do:** walk a fraud victim in Mexico (a transfer to an impostor, a hijacked WhatsApp, invoices in her name at SAT, a card charge she didn't make) through the next 7 days, with the papers ready, so she reaches each institution once and in the right order.

**For whom:** the person who lost the money or the account, sent by her own bank once the urgent report has gone in. Day one is **the sister**: *"Le transferí 8,000 a mi hermano y no era él."*

**What it will never do:**
1. **Hold what it protects.** No name, CURP, RFC, INE, photo or phone number on our server. The documents are filled in and printed **in her browser**.
2. **Write first.** No message, call, SMS or email from us, ever, not even a follow-up. Reminders are a calendar file on her own phone.
3. **Ask for** a card number, NIP, codes, passwords, transfers, or to install anything.
4. **Answer "were you exposed?" about a person.** There is no CURP lookup. With 195M records leaked, the answer for everyone is "assume yes."
5. **Act on her behalf.** It coaches; she signs, she files.
6. **Handle what moves in minutes:** money leaving right now goes to her bank's line, and threats go to 911/089, never to a bot.
7. **Advise her in a dispute against the bank that pays us.** It hands her a prefilled CONDUSEF complaint instead and counts it publicly.
8. **Let the model read what she types.** The LLM only drafts the fixed playbook, a human approves each draft, and it's served as static text.

**Long view (3 sentences):** If this slice works, in three years Respaldo is the after-fraud coach that every Mexican bank links from its "operación no reconocida" screen, paid per case by contact centers and shared through the ABM so the sister's bank and the mule's bank send people to the same place. When Mexico copies the UK's reimbursement rule (the Reversibility Line), the step-completion data, which records step states only and no identities, becomes the evidence banks need to show they moved fast. The load-bearing walls never move: we hold no identity, we never write first, and complaints against our payer are always public.
